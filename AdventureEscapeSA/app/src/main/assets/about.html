About Us

Adventure Escape SA was established in 2024 and operates throughout the Western Cape. We provide professionally guided outdoor experiences for visitors of all ages and experience levels.

Our founder, Liam Daniels, has always had a passion for outdoor adventure and eco-tourism. He noticed that many families, tourists, schools, and corporate groups struggle to find one place where they can compare and book exciting outdoor activities. That is why Adventure Escape SA was created.

Who is it for?

Individuals, families, schools, tourists, and corporate groups can all book with us.

Discounts

Customers may select more than one booking. The more bookings they make, the bigger the discount:

  • One booking - no discount
  • Two bookings - 5% discount
  • Three bookings - 10% discount
  • More than three bookings - 15% discount
