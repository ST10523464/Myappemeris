1.App Name
 Adventure Escape SA –
Founded in 2024 by Liam Daniels. Bringing families, tourists, schools, and corporate groups together
for guided outdoor experiences across the Western Cape.
2. App Overview
 The app is basically an alternative that brings together outdoor people (individuals, schools,
families, tourists, corporate groups) who have been searching for an exciting place to engage
in adventure, as the app consists of packages that accommodate such needs (adventure
package and individual package) with great prices and discounts that make the area more
alluring to the human eye.
3. Features
 The main function of the app is to get as many bookings as possible to bring together a
variety of guided outdoor experiences that encourage teamwork, fitness and a love of
nature.
4. Screens
 The main screens of the app are the product screens where the exhilarating packages are,
and the payment screen where there&#39;s an inclusion of discounts with the package chosen.
